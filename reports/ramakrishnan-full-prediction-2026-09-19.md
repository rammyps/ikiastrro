### Saturn — currently in Pisces (H12) since 29 Mar 2025

- **Now → 2 Jun 2027:** Saturn transits **Pisces, the 12th house** — 5th house *from the Moon*
  (not a Sade Sati window; Sade Sati requires the 12th/1st/2nd from Moon, i.e. Libra/Scorpio/
  Sagittarius, which Saturn will not reach again this decade). 12th-house themes: foreign
  connection, withdrawal/retreat, expenses, sleep and subconscious patterns, closing out old
  obligations. Ashtakavarga bindus here are only **3** — below the supportive threshold, so this
  transit runs with more friction and slower payoff than its placid house-meaning suggests.
- **This currently overlaps the Saturn Pratyantardasha (14 Sep 2026 – 6 Mar 2027)** — a
  transit-to-dasha resonance ("Saturn on Saturn") that tends to intensify Saturn's own
  significations for those six months: contraction, discipline, and quiet consolidation rather
  than expansion. Treat this window as better suited to closing loops than starting new ventures.
- **2 Jun 2027 → 20 Oct 2027, then retrograde back to Pisces (20 Oct 2027 – 23 Feb 2028), then
  direct into Aries again from 23 Feb 2028:** Saturn crosses onto the **natal Ascendant itself
  (Aries, H1)** — 6th house *from the Moon*. This is a mixed signal worth stating precisely:
  - Against the person: Saturn transiting one's own Lagna sign is classically a demanding period
    for body, self-image, and personal energy — everything feels heavier and slower — and
    Ashtakavarga bindus in Aries are also weak (**3**).
  - For the person: 6th-from-Moon is one of Saturn's three classically *favourable* Chandra
    Gochara positions (3rd/6th/11th) — good for competing, working through health regimens, and
    converting effort into results, especially at work.
  - Net read: a "grind now, gain through the grind" period rather than a purely difficult one —
    consistent with Saturn also being this chart's #2 strength (best Ishta/Kashta ratio, §3).
  - This Aries ingress also lands inside the **Mercury Pratyantardasha (6 Mar – 8 Aug 2027)**,
    so early 2027's communication/analysis-themed sub-period runs right into Saturn's arrival on
    the Ascendant — expect that period to demand more self-discipline and follow-through than
    Mercury sub-periods usually do.

### Jupiter — currently in Cancer (H4, its own exaltation sign) since 1 Jun 2026

- **Now → 31 Oct 2026:** Jupiter transits **Cancer, the 4th house, exalted** — and this is the
  **9th house from the Moon**, one of Jupiter's classically best Gochara positions (2nd/5th/7th/
  9th/11th from Moon). Exaltation + 9th-from-Moon together make this one of the most favourable
  Jupiter transits this chart will see: home, mother, property, domestic peace, and a general
  sense of fortune/support are all activated. Ashtakavarga bindus: **6** in Cancer — well above
  the supportive threshold, corroborating the classical reading.
- **31 Oct 2026 → 24 Jan 2027:** moves direct into **Leo (H5)** — children, intelligence,
  speculation, creative output. Bindus: **4**, adequate.
- **24 Jan 2027 → 25 Jun 2027:** retrogrades back into **Cancer (H4)**, repeating the exalted/
  9th-from-Moon window above — a second pass at the same favourable domestic/fortune theme.
- **25 Jun 2027 → 26 Nov 2027:** direct again into **Leo (H5)**.
- **26 Nov 2027 → 28 Feb 2028, retrograde to Leo, then direct again 24 Jul 2028 → 26 Dec 2028:**
  Jupiter enters **Virgo (H6)** — this is Jupiter's **own natal sign and house**. A transiting
  return to one's own natal Jupiter placement is a recognized ~12-year marker; here it lands
  around age 46–47 and falls on the 6th house (service, health routines, debt, daily work) that
  Jupiter itself rules the 9th/12th from. Bindus: **5**, supportive. Expect this to read as a
  cycle-closing, recommitment-to-routine period rather than a dramatic one.
- All of this sits inside the **Rahu Antardasha (through 7 Nov 2028)** — Jupiter, as 9th/12th
  lord, transiting favourably through Cancer/Leo/Virgo during a Rahu Antardasha tends to express
  as opportunity that arrives through unconventional, foreign, or networked channels rather than
  conventional ones, consistent with Rahu's own significations.
