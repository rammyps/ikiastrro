Design notes

Header & layout

  • App bar: HOME is the left-most item; then the nav tabs TRANSIT · ALL CHARTS · KEY INFERENCE (caps); the brand lockup sits on the right. The nav tabs are hidden until a person is opened — all three inner pages are per-person.
  • Compact band: page title + descriptor (caps, left) · person name + birth (centre) · meta chips (right, the 21 charts / 611 rows chip removed). On Home the centre + right of the band are empty — no person is picked yet.
  • One content width (--maxw 1280px) and one gutter (--pad-x) across app bar, band, pages and footer.
  • Footer: a single centred line — Dedicated to my guru (Sundari Hemachandran) — By Ramakrishnan P.

Home

  • Band heading is DISCOVER YOUR PATH, no sub-heading. All explanatory copy removed — just the search field, results and ＋ Add New. Home text is midnight blue (not muted).
  • A search, not a list. Typing a name shows the match as one row (name · birth · View chart →).
  • HOME is pulled into the left margin (centred in the gutter, vertically centred).
  • Ganesha / Navagraha illustration in the right column (placeholder ring here).

Transit — D1 Birth Chart (landing)

  • Band heading TRANSIT - D1 BIRTH CHART; the in-page "Transit" strip is removed.
  • Left: the real transit-wheel SVG (UI_SVG_Templates/ikiastrro-transit-wheel.svg, embedded) in place of the D1 grid.
  • Right: a table with two tabs — D1 Birth (House · Planet · Motion · Degree · Sign · Nakṣatra · Nak. Pad) and Current Transit (House from D1 = houses between the graha's natal and transit house · Planet · Motion · Degree · Speed °/day · In sign since · In-sign motion † · Next change · Next-change motion †).
  • Both tables sorted by how long a graha holds a house: Saturn → Jupiter → Rahu → Ketu → Mars → Venus → Mercury → Moon → Sun (Lagna first on D1).
  • † DB change needed: the DB holds current motion only for the transit chart. Two new fields — InSignMotion (motion when the graha entered its current sign) and NextChangeMotion (motion at the next sign change). Recorded in docs/ui/components/transit.md.

All Charts (ready to lock)

  • Plain CSS South-Indian grid, square cells, 3 per row. Only the card heading bar is sunset; card, grid and margins are on the canvas; heading text midnight blue.
  • Full sign name top-left; house from Lagna over house from Moon top-right. Graha glyphs colour-coded; LAGNA label; Lagna box = peach fill + sunset corner tick.

Page 2 — Key Inference : 4 headers

  • KEY INFERENCE — 8 sub-tabs: About Sign · About Planet · About Moon · About Houses · Planet Dignity · Planet Strength (Shadbala) · House Strength (Bhava Bala) · Vargottama (D1 & D9). The chart dropdown shows on About Houses and Planet Dignity (both chart-scoped). Combust planets sort directly under the Sun in Planet Dignity.
  • YOGAS — coverage summary + source variants.
  • TIME PERIOD (DASHA) — Vimśottari drill-down: Mahādaśā → Antardaśā → Pratyantardaśā, each expandable. On load the currently-running chain (1st → 2nd → 3rd level) is expanded and highlighted in sunset.
  • SATURN TIME PERIOD — Sade Sati (3 dhaiyas) + Kaṇṭaka Śani + Aṣṭama Śani in one table, ascending from birth to age 75. New Round column (left of “From Moon”) with 1st / 2nd / 3rd round filters. The current or next window is highlighted in sunset. Ashtakavarga stays out of scope (calculations.md §9).
